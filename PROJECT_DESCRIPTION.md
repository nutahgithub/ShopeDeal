# ShopeDeal - Mo ta du an va dinh huong cong nghe

## 1. Tong quan y tuong

ShopeDeal la web app ho tro nguoi dung tim san pham dang mua tren Shopee bang cach so sanh gia, danh gia, voucher va lich su gia. Muc tieu cua san pham la giup nguoi mua tiet kiem thoi gian, tranh mua nham deal kem, va nhan thong bao dung luc khi co gia tot hoac dot sale lon.

San pham khong nen dinh vi la cong cu "mua ho" hay tu dong dat hang thay nguoi dung trong giai doan dau. Huong phu hop hon la tro ly san deal thong minh, dan nguoi dung sang Shopee bang affiliate link khi ho quyet dinh mua.

## 2. Doi tuong nguoi dung

- Nguoi mua hang Shopee thuong xuyen va muon san gia tot.
- Nguoi hay mua do cong nghe, gia dung, my pham, thoi trang, me va be.
- Nguoi khong muon tu doc qua nhieu review va so sanh nhieu shop.
- Nhom san deal tren Facebook, Telegram, Zalo.
- Ve sau co the mo rong sang seller can theo doi gia doi thu va xu huong san pham.

## 3. Gia tri cot loi

- Tim san pham dang mua nhanh hon.
- Biet luc nao nen mua, luc nao nen doi sale.
- Nhan dien shop/san pham co rui ro tu review, rating va lich su ban hang.
- Goi y cach ap dung voucher, freeship va uu dai de co tong gia tot hon.
- Tao nguon doanh thu qua affiliate ma van giu trai nghiem huu ich cho nguoi dung.

## 4. MVP de xuat

### 4.1. Tim kiem va so sanh san pham

Nguoi dung co the nhap tu khoa hoac dan link san pham Shopee. He thong tra ve danh sach san pham lien quan va sap xep theo do dang mua.

Thong tin can hien thi:

- Ten san pham.
- Anh san pham.
- Gia hien tai.
- Gia sau voucher neu co the tinh duoc.
- Phi van chuyen uoc tinh neu co du lieu.
- Rating trung binh.
- So luot ban.
- Ten shop.
- Diem uy tin shop.
- Link mua tren Shopee.

### 4.2. Cham diem deal

Moi san pham duoc cham mot diem tong hop, vi du tu 0 den 100.

Cac yeu to nen tinh diem:

- Gia so voi cac san pham tuong tu.
- Gia so voi lich su gia.
- Rating va so luong danh gia.
- So luot ban.
- Do uy tin cua shop.
- Voucher/freeship kha dung.
- Rui ro review ao hoac thong tin bat thuong.

Ket qua hien thi nen don gian:

- "Rat dang mua"
- "Co the mua"
- "Nen can nhac"
- "Rui ro cao"

### 4.3. Tom tat danh gia bang AI

He thong doc danh gia san pham va tao tom tat ngan gon cho nguoi dung.

Noi dung tom tat:

- Diem manh duoc nhac lai nhieu.
- Diem yeu duoc nhac lai nhieu.
- Van de thuong gap.
- Danh gia ve chat luong, dong goi, giao hang, dung mo ta.
- Canh bao neu review co dau hieu bat thuong.

### 4.4. Theo doi gia va thong bao

Nguoi dung co the theo doi mot san pham va dat muc gia mong muon.

He thong gui thong bao khi:

- Gia giam duoi muc nguoi dung dat.
- San pham co voucher moi.
- Shop tham gia dot sale.
- San pham sap het hang.
- Sap den ngay sale lon nhu 6.6, 9.9, 11.11, 12.12.

Kenh thong bao giai doan dau:

- Email.
- Web notification.
- Telegram bot hoac Zalo OA o giai doan sau.

### 4.5. Goi y voucher va ma khuyen mai

He thong hien thi cac uu dai co the ap dung cho san pham.

Chuc nang ban dau:

- Liet ke voucher shop.
- Liet ke voucher san neu co du lieu.
- Goi y freeship.
- Hien thi gia uoc tinh sau uu dai.

Giai doan sau:

- Tu dong tim combo voucher tot nhat.
- Canh bao voucher sap het han hoac sap het luot.

### 4.6. Affiliate link

Khi nguoi dung bam "Mua tren Shopee", he thong chuyen sang Shopee bang affiliate link neu co the.

Luu y:

- Can tuan thu chinh sach affiliate cua Shopee.
- Khong nen an giau viec co link tiep thi lien ket neu can minh bach voi nguoi dung.
- Khong nen tu dong dat hang, tu dong dang nhap, hoac can thiep vao qua trinh thanh toan.

## 5. Chuc nang sau MVP

- Canh bao khi doi thu giam gia.

Chuc nang phu hop tren mobile:

## 6. Mo hinh kiem tien

### 6.1. Affiliate commission

Nguon thu chinh. Nguoi dung bam link mua hang tren Shopee, he thong nhan hoa hong neu don hang hop le theo chuong trinh affiliate.

### 6.2. Goi premium cho nguoi mua

Co the thu phi hang thang cho cac tinh nang nang cao:

- Theo doi nhieu san pham hon.
- Canh bao gia nhanh hon.
- Lich su gia dai hon.
- Tom tat review bang AI khong gioi han.
- Goi y voucher nang cao.

Gia tham khao:

- Free: theo doi gioi han, tinh nang co ban.
- Pro: 29.000 - 59.000 VND/thang.
- Power user: 99.000 VND/thang.

### 6.3. Quang cao va deal tai tro

Shop hoac brand co the tra phi de hien thi deal tai tro.

Can dam bao:

- Co nhan "Tai tro".
- Khong tron voi ket qua xep hang tu nhien.
- Van bao ve niem tin cua nguoi dung.

### 6.4. B2B SaaS cho seller

Nguon thu tiem nang cao hon ve sau:

- Goi theo doi doi thu.
- Bao cao nganh hang.
- Canh bao bien dong gia.
- Goi y chien dich sale.

## 7. Rui ro va luu y

### 7.1. Du lieu Shopee

Can uu tien nguon du lieu hop le:

- Shopee Open Platform neu duoc cap quyen.
- Shopee Affiliate/Partner tools.
- Du lieu do nguoi dung cung cap qua link.
- Extension hoat dong tren trinh duyet nguoi dung neu phu hop voi chinh sach.

Can tranh crawl qua muc hoac vi pham dieu khoan nen tang.

### 7.2. Do chinh xac cua gia va voucher

Gia, ton kho va voucher thay doi nhanh. He thong can hien thi thoi diem cap nhat gan nhat va tranh cam ket qua manh.

### 7.3. Review ao va du lieu nhieu

AI co the tom tat va canh bao rui ro, nhung khong nen ket luan tuyet doi. Nen dung ngon ngu nhu "co dau hieu", "nen can nhac", "chua du du lieu".

### 7.4. Tu dong mua hang

Khong nen lam trong MVP. Tu dong checkout co the lien quan den tai khoan, thanh toan, OTP, bao mat va chinh sach nen tang.

## 8. Cong nghe de xuat

### 8.1. Huong khuyen nghi cho MVP

Stack de xuat:

- Frontend: Next.js, React, TypeScript.
- Styling: Tailwind CSS.
- Backend: Next.js API Routes hoac NestJS neu muon tach backend rieng.
- Database: PostgreSQL.
- ORM: Prisma.
- Cache/Queue: Redis.
- Background jobs: BullMQ.
- Authentication: NextAuth/Auth.js.
- AI summary: OpenAI API hoac model AI tuong duong.
- Search: PostgreSQL full-text search ban dau, sau do nang cap Elasticsearch/Meilisearch.
- Hosting: Vercel cho frontend, Railway/Fly.io/Render cho backend va database.
- Monitoring: Sentry.
- Analytics: PostHog hoac Plausible.

Ly do chon:

- Next.js giup lam nhanh web app, SEO tot cho cac trang deal.
- TypeScript giup giam loi khi du an lon dan.
- PostgreSQL phu hop luu san pham, gia, user, tracking, voucher.
- Redis + BullMQ phu hop cho tac vu nen nhu cap nhat gia, gui thong bao, xu ly review.
- Prisma giup lam viec voi database nhanh va de bao tri.

### 8.2. Kien truc tong quan

Thanh phan chinh:

- Web app: giao dien nguoi dung, tim kiem, xem deal, theo doi san pham.
- API server: xu ly business logic, user, san pham, affiliate link.
- Worker: cap nhat gia, lay voucher, gui thong bao, tom tat review.
- Database: luu user, product, shop, price history, alert, voucher, click tracking.
- Cache: luu ket qua tim kiem va san pham hot de tang toc.
- Notification service: email, web push, Telegram/Zalo ve sau.

### 8.3. Cau truc database goi y

Bang ban dau:

- users
- products
- shops
- product_snapshots
- price_history
- reviews
- review_summaries
- vouchers
- user_watchlists
- price_alerts
- affiliate_clicks
- sale_events

### 8.4. Cong nghe nen can nhac sau MVP

- Chrome Extension: React + TypeScript + Plasmo.
- Mobile app: React Native hoac Expo.
- Search nang cao: Meilisearch hoac Elasticsearch.
- Data pipeline: Python cho crawler/ETL neu can xu ly du lieu rieng.
- Data warehouse: BigQuery/Supabase/ClickHouse neu traffic va du lieu lon.
- Recommendation engine: vector search hoac ranking model rieng.
